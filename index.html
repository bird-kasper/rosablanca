About

A florist and gift shop in the heart of Suburbs

Rosa Blanca is a florist and gift shop on Seventh Street in Suburbs, Bulawayo. We arrange flowers for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries and farewells, and pair them with gifts from the shop.

Orders can be collected in store, delivered within Zimbabwe, or sent internationally. Message us on WhatsApp and we will talk through the colours, the flowers and the timing.
